Clipwright

Clipwright is a personal publishing tool. Its operator uses it to turn long videos into short vertical clips, review each one, and post the approved clips on a schedule to the operator's own YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram accounts, through each platform's official API.

It runs only on the operator's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and it has no server.
